06 / Quantitative Analysis

Scientific Data Analysis

My research involves computational analysis and interpretation of experimental measurements using MATLAB, spectral fitting, statistical methods, and kinetic analysis. I develop and evaluate physics-guided descriptors to investigate relationships between material properties and functional performance.

01 / DATA PROCESSING

From experimental measurements to usable data

Experimental datasets provide the starting point for quantitative interpretation. My analysis connects measured signals with clearly defined quantities that can be compared across samples and experimental conditions.

MATLAB-based analysis

I use MATLAB to organise measurements, perform calculations, fit models, and visualise results. Script-based analysis supports consistent treatment of datasets and makes the sequence of calculations easier to review.

Preparing measurements for comparison

  • Data organisation: retain sample identities, measurement conditions, and the meaning of each recorded variable.
  • Units and scales: check conversions and maintain consistent physical units.
  • Background treatment: apply appropriate corrections while preserving the original measurements.
  • Normalization: state the reference quantity used when comparing signals of different magnitudes.

Any smoothing, filtering, or exclusion of data should be justified and documented because these choices can affect subsequent interpretation.

  • MATLAB
  • Data organisation
  • Normalization
  • Visualisation
02 / QUANTITATIVE METHODS

Spectral fitting, statistics, and kinetics

Quantitative methods help extract information from experimental curves and spectra. The choice of method depends on the measurement, the scientific question, and the assumptions supported by the data.

Spectral fitting

Fitting can describe spectral features through quantities such as peak position, width, and area. Background selection, line shape, and fitting constraints influence these estimates and should be considered when comparing samples.

Statistical analysis

Statistical methods support the description of variability, comparison of measurements, and assessment of relationships between experimental quantities.

  • Summary statistics: describe central values and the spread of repeated measurements.
  • Regression: examine relationships between variables within a stated model.
  • Uncertainty: consider measurement variation and uncertainty in fitted parameters.

Kinetic analysis

Time-dependent measurements can be analysed using suitable kinetic relationships to estimate rates and characteristic times. A good fit alone does not establish the underlying physical mechanism.

  • Spectral fitting
  • Statistics
  • Regression
  • Kinetic analysis
03 / MODELLING AND INTERPRETATION

Physics-guided descriptors and material relationships

I develop and evaluate descriptors that connect material characteristics with measured behaviour. The aim is to express experimentally relevant relationships using quantities grounded in the physical context of the problem.

Choosing meaningful descriptors

A useful descriptor has a clear definition, consistent units, and an interpretable relationship with the phenomenon being studied. Its relevance should be evaluated against the available experimental evidence.

Evaluating a model

  • Physical consistency: check whether assumptions, parameter values, and units are reasonable.
  • Residual behaviour: examine differences between observations and model predictions for systematic patterns.
  • Parameter sensitivity: assess how analysis choices and measurement variation influence the results.
  • Scope of interpretation: distinguish relationships supported by the measured range from extrapolations.

Connecting structure with performance

Combining material characterization with quantitative measurements helps investigate relationships between processing, structure, surface chemistry, and functional behaviour. Correlations provide evidence for further investigation rather than proof of causation.

  • Physics-guided modelling
  • Descriptors
  • Model evaluation
  • Structure–property relationships
