06 / Quantitative analysis

Measurements.
Models. Meaning.

I use MATLAB, spectral fitting, statistical methods, and kinetic analysis to interpret experimental data and investigate material relationships.

Analysis notebook

The working sequence

01 · Prepare the data

02 · Extract quantities

03 · Evaluate the model

04 · Interpret the evidence

Processing choices and assumptions should remain traceable to the original measurements.

Entry 01 / Preparation

Keep the measurement context.

Sample identity, experimental conditions, units, and the meaning of each variable are part of the dataset. MATLAB supports organisation, calculations, fitting, and visualization through a consistent analysis sequence.

Background correction, normalization, and filtering need clear justification. Original measurements should be preserved alongside processed data.

Entry 02 / Quantification

Ask what can be extracted.

Spectral fitting describes features such as peak position, width, and area. Background, line shape, and constraints influence the estimates.

Statistics helps describe variability and relationships between measured quantities. Kinetic analysis uses suitable time-dependent models to examine rates and characteristic times.

Entry 03 / Model checks

A good fit is a starting point.

  • Check the units and physical plausibility of parameters.
  • Examine residuals for systematic patterns.
  • Assess sensitivity to analysis choices and measurement variation.
  • Distinguish interpolation from extrapolation.

A fitted curve alone does not establish the physical mechanism.

Entry 04 / Interpretation

Build physically meaningful relationships.

I develop and evaluate physics-guided descriptors to investigate connections between material properties and functional performance. A descriptor needs a clear definition, consistent units, and an interpretation supported by experimental evidence.

Correlations can guide further investigation; they do not, on their own, establish causation.

The goal: turn experimental measurements into interpretable evidence, with the assumptions and limitations still visible.
