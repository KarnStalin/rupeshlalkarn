Research interests

My research focuses on understanding the relationships between material processing, structure, surface chemistry, and functional performance. My work integrates experimental characterization, quantitative analysis, and physics-guided modelling, with applications in sustainable energy, functional materials, and engineering.

Two-dimensional materials

MXenes & Functional Composites

Synthesis, processing, and characterization of titanium-based MXenes, including selective etching, DMSO intercalation, and transition-metal sulfide composites.

Investigation of processing-induced changes in structure, surface chemistry, morphology, and elemental composition.
