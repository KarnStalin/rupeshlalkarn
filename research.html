Research Interests

My research focuses on understanding the relationships between material processing, structure, surface chemistry, and functional performance. My work integrates experimental characterization, quantitative analysis, and physics-guided approaches, with applications in sustainable energy, functional materials, and engineering.

01 / TWO-DIMENSIONAL MATERIALS

MXenes & Functional Composites

Synthesis, processing, and characterization of titanium-based MXenes, including acid etching, DMSO intercalation, and transition-metal chalcogenide composites. Investigation of processing-induced structural changes and surface chemistry using complementary characterization techniques.

MXenes XRD Raman XPS
Explore research → 02 / PHOTOTHERMAL CONVERSION

Laser-Induced Heating

Experimental investigation of photothermal heating in MAX phases, acid-etched MXenes, and intercalated MXenes under near-infrared irradiation. Quantitative evaluation of temperature evolution and net photothermal heat generation.

Photothermal Heat Generation MATLAB
Explore research → 03 / ELECTROCHEMISTRY

Electrocatalytic Materials

Electrochemical characterization of functional materials for energy-related applications, including oxygen evolution reaction activity, charge-transfer resistance, and electrochemical kinetics. Analysis using voltammetry, impedance spectroscopy, and Tafel relationships.

LSV EIS Tafel OER
Explore research → 04 / MAGNETIC MATERIALS

Complex Magnetic Oxides

Structural and magnetic investigations of complex oxide materials using X-ray diffraction, Rietveld refinement, Raman spectroscopy, and magnetic measurements. Exploration of relationships between crystal structure and magnetic behavior.

Magnetism Rietveld Raman PPMS
Explore research → 05 / SUSTAINABLE ENERGY

Alternative Fuels & Nanoparticles

Experimental investigations of biodiesel, waste plastic pyrolysis oil, and nanoparticle-enhanced fuel blends. Evaluation of combustion characteristics, engine performance, and emissions in internal combustion engines.

Biodiesel Plastic Fuel Combustion and Emission Nanoparticles
Explore research → 06 / QUANTITATIVE ANALYSIS

Scientific Data Analysis

Computational analysis and interpretation of experimental measurements using MATLAB, spectral fitting, statistical methods, and kinetic analysis. Development and evaluation of physics-guided descriptors to investigate relationships between material properties and functional performance.
