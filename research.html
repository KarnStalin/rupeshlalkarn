Research Interests

My research focuses on understanding the relationships between material processing, structure, surface chemistry, and functional performance, with applications in sustainable energy, advanced materials, and engineering.

01 / TWO-DIMENSIONAL MATERIALS

MXenes & Functional Composites

Synthesis and characterization of titanium-based MXenes, including acid etching, DMSO intercalation, and transition-metal chalcogenide composites.

MXenes XRD Raman XPS
02 / PHOTOTHERMAL CONVERSION

Laser-Induced Heating

Investigation of the photothermal response of MAX phases, acid-etched MXenes, and intercalated MXenes under near-infrared irradiation.

Photothermal Heat Generation MATLAB
03 / ELECTROCHEMISTRY

Electrocatalytic Materials

Electrochemical characterization of functional materials, including oxygen evolution reaction activity, charge-transfer behavior, and reaction kinetics.

LSV EIS Tafel OER
04 / MAGNETIC MATERIALS

Complex Magnetic Oxides

Structural and magnetic investigations of complex oxides using diffraction, Raman spectroscopy, and magnetic measurement techniques.

Magnetism Rietveld PPMS
05 / SUSTAINABLE ENERGY

Alternative Fuels & Nanoparticles

Experimental studies involving biodiesel, waste plastic pyrolysis oil, nanoparticle additives, combustion, engine performance, and emissions.

Biodiesel Combustion Nanoparticles
06 / QUANTITATIVE ANALYSIS

Scientific Data Analysis

Analysis of experimental results using MATLAB, spectral fitting, statistical methods, kinetic interpretation, and physics-guided descriptors.

MATLAB Modeling Statistics
