Alternative Fuels & Nanoparticles

Fuel preparation, characterization, and engine testing across biodiesel, plastic-derived fuels, and nanoparticle-containing blends.

My work connects the composition and properties of alternative fuels with their performance, combustion behaviour, and exhaust response. The research includes laboratory fuel preparation, comparative engine measurements, and analysis of experimental results.

01

Biodiesel

Preparation from plant oils, animal fat, and waste cooking oil, followed by fuel-property evaluation and blend studies.

→ 02

Plastic-Derived Fuels

Waste-plastic pyrolysis, liquid recovery, filtration, and fractional distillation, with original laboratory photographs.

→ 03

Combustion & Emissions

Research-engine instrumentation, combustion analysis, fuel consumption, exhaust-gas measurements, and smoke-opacity testing.

→ 04

Nanoparticle Fuel Additives

Studies of ZnO and CuO additives in alternative-fuel blends, with links to the associated peer-reviewed publications.

Experimental Scope

From Fuel Properties to Engine Response

Fuel composition, blend fraction, engine loading, and measurement conditions provide the basis for comparing each fuel with its reference. The measurements available differ between projects.

Fuel Characterization

Density, viscosity, calorific value, flash and pour points, and chemical characterization where available.

Performance & Combustion

Power, thermal efficiency, specific fuel consumption, cylinder-pressure response, and heat-release analysis.

Exhaust Measurements

Smoke opacity and gas-specific emissions are interpreted according to the instruments and scope of the individual study.
