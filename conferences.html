Scientific Poster

Processing-Induced Structural and Surface-Chemical Evolution in Ti₃AlCN-Derived Ti₃CNTₓ

10th Annual Texas A&M Conference on Energy
College Station, Texas, USA

Authors: Rupesh Lal Karn, Gururaj M. Neelgund, and Raghava R. Kommalapati

This research investigates processing-induced structural and surface-chemical changes in Ti₃AlCN-derived Ti₃CNTₓ MXenes. The work combines XRD, Raman spectroscopy, FTIR, XPS, BET analysis, and photothermal measurements with physics-guided predictive modeling.
