01 / ENERGY CONVERSION

Combustion testing

Combustion testing examines how fuel burns inside an engine and how chemical energy is converted into useful work. Cylinder pressure recorded against crank angle can be used to study combustion timing, pressure development, and heat-release behaviour.

Engine speed, load, and fuel consumption provide additional context for comparing operating conditions and evaluating alternative fuel blends.

02 / EXHAUST CHARACTERISATION

Emission and opacity testing

Emission testing examines engine exhaust to understand the environmental effects of fuel use. Depending on the analyser, measurements may include carbon monoxide, hydrocarbons, carbon dioxide, and nitrogen oxides.

Smoke opacity testing measures how much light is blocked by an exhaust sample. It helps assess smoke behaviour and complements gas measurements; it is not a direct measurement of particulate mass.

Connecting combustion with emissions

Combustion and exhaust measurements provide complementary views of engine operation. Examining them together helps assess the relationships between fuel properties, operating conditions, energy conversion, and pollutant formation.
