Nanoparticle Fuel Additives

Experimental studies of nanoparticle-containing alternative fuels and their effects on engine performance, combustion, and emissions.

Overview Biodiesel Plastic Fuel Combustion and Emission Nanoparticles

Research Focus

My work includes green-synthesized ZnO additives in rice-bran-oil biodiesel blends and CuO nanoparticles in pine oil–diesel blends. These studies examine the relationship between additive-containing fuels and measured engine response.
