About Me

I am a graduate researcher in Engineering at Prairie View A&M University, specializing in experimental materials research, photothermal conversion, electrochemistry, and the characterization of functional nanomaterials.

My academic journey began with Automobile Engineering at the Institute of Engineering, Tribhuvan University, Nepal. My earlier research focused on biodiesel, alternative fuels, nanoparticle additives, engine performance, combustion, and emissions.

My current research explores the relationship between material processing, structure, surface chemistry, and functional performance, particularly in MXenes and related materials.
