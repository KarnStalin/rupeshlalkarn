About & Education

Graduate researcher in Engineering at Prairie View A&M University, working on functional materials, performance modelling, crystal growth, and magnetism.

Research background

My research examines how synthesis and processing change material structure and interfaces, and how those changes relate to physical response. I combine experimental characterization with physics-based modelling to study these relationships.

My work with MXenes and related composites combines synthesis, processing, and characterization. I use MATLAB-supported thermal, photothermal, and electrochemical modelling to extract parameters and build interpretable links between measurements and response.

My magnetic-materials research includes magnetic-powder synthesis, cobalt substitution in Mn1−xCoxGeTeO6 , and growth of Mn3Al2Ge3O12 single crystals. Visiting research appointments at Argonne and Los Alamos National Laboratories extended my experience in magnetic-domain analysis and single-crystal measurements.

Before my graduate work, I studied Automobile Engineering at Thapathali Campus, Institute of Engineering, Tribhuvan University, Nepal. My earlier research covered biodiesel, plastic-derived fuels, nanoparticle additives, and engine performance, combustion, and emissions.
