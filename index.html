01 / About

Research driven by curiosity.

I am a graduate researcher in Engineering at Prairie View A&M University, with an academic background in Automobile Engineering.

My research interests include two-dimensional materials, MXenes, functional nanomaterials, photothermal conversion, electrochemical processes, magnetic materials, and sustainable energy technologies.

My work combines experimental investigations with materials characterization, quantitative analysis, and engineering-based interpretation of material behavior.

Before my graduate studies in the United States, I worked on alternative fuels, biodiesel, nanoparticle additives, and internal combustion engines in Nepal.
