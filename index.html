01 / Introduction

Research at the intersection
of disciplines.

My work spans layered MXenes, nanostructured functional materials, transition-metal compounds, magnetic oxides, and energy-related systems. I combine synthesis and experimental characterization with quantitative, physics-guided analysis to investigate structure–property relationships.

My current interests include photothermal conversion, electrochemical behavior, surface chemistry, thermal stability, and descriptor-based modeling. Earlier work focused on alternative fuels and nanoparticle additives for internal-combustion engines.

Beyond the laboratory, I have taught engineering courses and supervised undergraduate thesis projects in Nepal.

02 / Research focus

Questions that drive my work.

From synthesis to characterization to modeling, I investigate how processing choices influence material behavior.

01 / 2D MATERIALS

MXenes & layered materials

Etching, intercalation, surface terminations, nanocomposites, and processing-induced structural changes.

02 / ENERGY CONVERSION

Photothermal response

Light-to-heat conversion, heating–cooling behavior, and experimental heat-generation analysis.

03 / ELECTROCHEMISTRY

Electrocatalysis & interfaces

LSV, EIS, Tafel analysis, cyclic voltammetry, and charge-transfer behavior of functional materials.

04 / DATA & MECHANISMS

Physics-guided descriptors

Linking XRD, Raman, FTIR, XPS, BET, and TGA-derived descriptors with measured performance.

05 / CONDENSED MATTER

Magnetic oxides & crystals

Crystal synthesis, structural refinement, magnetic domain characterization, and quantum-oscillation measurements.

06 / SUSTAINABLE FUELS

Alternative fuels

Biodiesel, plastic pyrolysis fuels, nanoparticle additives, engine performance, and emissions.
