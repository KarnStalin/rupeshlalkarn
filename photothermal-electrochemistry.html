Photothermal & Electrochemical Studies

My experimental work examines how functional materials respond to light and to electrochemical conditions. Select a study area to explore its materials, measurements, and experimental approach.

Light & temperature

Photothermal Studies

Near-infrared irradiation provides a controlled way to examine heating and cooling in MAX, acid-etched MXene, and DMSO-treated MXene suspensions.

  • Temperature evolution during irradiation and after switch-off
  • Comparisons across processing states and suspension concentrations
  • Water-reference comparisons and finite-time heating measures

Charge & electrode interfaces

Electrochemical Studies

Complementary electrochemical measurements examine charge storage, impedance, and electrocatalytic response in functional materials for energy-related applications.

  • Cyclic voltammetry and galvanostatic charge–discharge
  • Impedance, interfacial response, and charge-transfer analysis
  • Polarization and Tafel analysis for oxygen-evolution studies
