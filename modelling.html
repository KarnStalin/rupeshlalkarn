Research / Modelling

Modelling & Descriptor Development

My modelling work connects material characterization with thermal, photothermal, and electrochemical behaviour through interpretable descriptors, physical equations, and numerical analysis.

MATLAB supports signal processing, parameter estimation, simulation, and model assessment. Each area below opens a dedicated page describing its methods, assumptions, and interpretation.

Characterization to variables

Descriptor Extraction

Processing diffraction, spectroscopy, gas-sorption, and dispersion measurements to obtain structural, chemical, interfacial, and hydrodynamic descriptors. Methods include baseline correction, peak fitting, band integration, and physically defined transformations.

XRD · XPS · Raman · FTIR · Gas sorption · SEM/EDX · Zeta potential · DLS

Thermal decomposition & conversion

TGA & Thermal Kinetics

Event-specific analysis of multi-rate TGA and DTG data, apparent activation-energy extraction, and numerical reconstruction of selected mass-loss events. The methods combine peak-based and isoconversional kinetics with an effective event model.

TGA / DTG · Kissinger · FWO · KAS · Starink · Friedman
Explore thermal modelling →
Irradiation-driven temperature response

Photothermal Modelling

Mathematical treatment of laser-induced heating and cooling using transient energy balances. Curve fitting, reference calibration, and time-constant analysis connect temperature measurements with heat-input estimates.

Energy balance · Heating / cooling · Time constants · Reference calibration
Explore photothermal modelling →
Charge storage & relaxation

Electrochemical Modelling

Complementary modelling of cyclic voltammetry, impedance spectroscopy, and galvanostatic charge–discharge. The framework combines scan-rate analysis, equivalent circuits, relaxation descriptions, and state-space simulation.

CV · EIS · GCD · Equivalent circuits · State-space models
Explore electrochemical modelling →
Connecting material descriptors with response

Physics-Guided Descriptor Modelling

Building interpretable relationships from characterization-derived variables. Descriptor screening, normalization, and composite-score construction connect structural, interfacial, vibrational, and dispersion information with physically motivated response functions.

Descriptor screening · Normalization · Composite scores · Concentration dependence · Sensitivity analysis · Uncertainty assessment
